03 / COORDINATION

When one trip has more moving parts.

Flights, rooms, transfers and activities rarely operate in isolation. We look at the timing between them so the itinerary works as one trip, not a collection of separate bookings.

The booking works better when the timings work together.
Once the details are clear, the request starts moving.
04 / REQUEST

How a request moves.

A practical route from the first trip details to confirmed arrangements.

HOW IT WORKS

One request can involve several bookings. We keep the details connected from the first brief through confirmation.

01

Start with the brief

Destination, dates, travelers and services.

02

Review the details

Timing, rooming, routing and open requirements.

03

Move to confirmation

Approved options are coordinated and documented.

05 / REAL REQUESTS

When the trip gets more complicated.

Some requests need more than a list of options. They need the details to move together.

01

Different arrival times

A group lands on several flights. We map each arrival to the right vehicle, hotel and rooming list.

02

A policy-led business trip

Hotel category, routing and timing are reviewed against the company brief before options are shared.

03

Several services, one itinerary

An agency combines accommodation, transfers and tours without managing three separate conversations.

04

A hospitality relationship

A property discusses booking access and commercial coordination with the team responsible for the relationship.

06 / WHY LEGENDARY

Coordination matters more than a long option list.

Clear communication matters in B2B travel. We make sure you know exactly what is happening with your requests.

  1. 01

    Single contact

    You talk to one team about all the services on your request.

  2. 02

    Aligned timing

    We coordinate the airport transfer with the hotel check-in and the scheduled activities.

  3. 03

    B2B focus

    Our communication assumes you are a travel professional who needs straight answers.

  4. 04

    Any scale

    Send us a simple hotel request or an entire group itinerary.

  5. 05

    Structured follow-up

    Passenger lists, rooming details and booking updates stay organized as the request moves.
