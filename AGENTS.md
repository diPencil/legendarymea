# LEGENDARY MANAGEMENT MEA - EDITORIAL RULE

## ENGLISH
Write like an experienced B2B travel professional, not a marketing generator.
- **Prefer:** concrete travel terminology, direct commercial language, natural sentence variation, useful information, specific use cases, concise copy.
- **Avoid:** generic corporate slogans, repetitive two-part headlines, abstract filler, unnecessary adjectives, SaaS language, rewriting the same idea with different words.
Every sentence should inform, clarify, differentiate, or help the visitor act.

## ARABIC
Arabic must be written independently in PROFESSIONAL SAUDI / GCC BUSINESS ARABIC.
Do NOT translate English sentence structure literally.
The Arabic should feel: واضح, عملي, مهني, طبيعي, تجاري بدون مبالغة.
- **Prefer natural phrasing:** نرتّب, نراجع معك, شاركنا التفاصيل, حسب طبيعة الطلب, حسب برنامج السفر, فريقك, شركتك, تفاصيل الحجز, الخدمات المطلوبة, مواعيد الرحلة, عرض السعر.
- **Avoid default corporate Arabic:** حلول متكاملة, مصممة لتلبية احتياجاتكم, تمكين النمو, تجربة سلسة, رؤية تشغيلية, تعظيم القيمة, شريك موثوق (unless the specific context genuinely requires it).
- **Do NOT** use Egyptian colloquial Arabic.
- **Do NOT** force Saudi slang.
The target is modern professional Saudi/GCC commercial Arabic.

## CONTENT DIFFERENTIATION
Do not repeat another page's message.
- Homepage = company introduction
- Solutions = travel capabilities
- About = identity and thinking
- Partners = commercial relationships
- Taxidia = product and technology
- FAQ = direct answers
- Contact = action

## FINAL SELF-CHECK
Before finishing any future copy:
1. Read English independently.
2. Then read Arabic WITHOUT looking at English.
If the Arabic only sounds correct because you know the English source, rewrite it.
